### 👋 About Us

Hi! We are **Team 화햇대장**, a team of 10 cybersecurity researchers participating in the 4th cohort of [WhiteHat School](https://www.whsedu.kr/), a cybersecurity talent development program in South Korea.

Our research focuses on **LLM-based software vulnerability detection** and discovering security vulnerabilities in open-source projects.

We follow **Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD)** practices to responsibly report our findings and contribute to a safer and more secure software ecosystem.
